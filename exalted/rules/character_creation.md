# Comprehensive Character Creation Guide — Exalted 2nd Edition (Solar)

Primary source: `Exalted 2E.pdf`. Page references appear inline as
`p.N`. Trait scale is **1–5 dots** (Essence and Willpower up to **10**).
Each dot equals one die added to the dice pool for that trait.

> **Errata from the prior summaries** (all corrected below, with
> citations):
> - **Zenith anima power** costs **10 motes** (not 5m) to activate the
>   light/soak effect; the corpse-burn touch is **1 mote per body**
>   (p.94).
> - **Twilight anima power** is **post-damage health-level subtraction**
>   for **5 motes** (subtract 1 HL per dot of Essence from a single
>   damage roll), **not** a flat `Essence × 2 L/B` soak shield (p.96).
> - **Eclipse oath sanctification** costs **10 motes + 1 Willpower**
>   (not 5m + 1wp) (p.100).
> - **Night anima muting** costs **+1m per non-Obvious Charm**, but
>   **double the normal mote cost for an Obvious Charm** (p.98).
> - **Anima display level 1–3** is "standard difficulty" for a
>   Perception + Awareness roll to spot the caste mark — the book does

## Step 1 — Concept (p.75)

1. **Concept** — one sentence describing who the character is. May
   reference background, occupation, and disposition (e.g. *"kidnapped
   by bandits as a child, Exalted as a teenager, transformed the gang
   into the nucleus of her conquering army"*). The book does **not**
   define a separate "Personality" field — disposition/temperament are
   subsumed into Concept; durable attachments belong in Intimacies.
2. **Caste** — pick one of five Solar castes. Determines Caste Abilities
   and anima power.
3. **Motivation** — one sentence describing the character's epic,
   long-term goal (full rules p.88). Distinct from Concept: Concept =
   who she is; Motivation = what she will move Creation to achieve.
4. **Anima Totem** — the burning totemic image that manifests at the
   16+ Peripheral display level (p.117). Player's free choice, not
   caste-constrained: "*surmounted or surrounded by a burning image
   totemic to her person*" — a warrior might rise inside a great golden
   bull, a Twilight magician inside an elaborate mandala. Record it
   here; mechanically irrelevant until iconic anima fires (§2).
5. Note the caste's **anima powers** and the universal Solar anima
   tricks (§1.6).

---

## Step 2 — Attributes (p.76)

- 9 Attributes in 3 categories: **Physical** (Strength, Dexterity,
  Stamina), **Social** (Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance), **Mental**
  (Perception, Intelligence, Wits).
- Every Attribute **starts at 1 dot free**.
- Prioritize categories: **Primary / Secondary / Tertiary** receive
  **8 / 6 / 4** additional dots distributed among the Attributes in
  that category.
- No Attribute may exceed **5** at creation, even with bonus points.

## Step 3 — Abilities (p.76–77)

- 25 Abilities total (5 per caste). All start at 0.
- Mark the 5 **Caste Abilities** from your caste.
- Pick **5 Favored Abilities**. Must NOT overlap with Caste Abilities.
- **28 Ability dots total**, distributed as follows:
  - At least **10 dots** must go into Caste or Favored Abilities.
  - At least **1 dot in each Favored Ability**.
  - No Ability may exceed **3** at creation (raising to 4 or 5 requires
    bonus points).
- Caste & Favored Abilities are discounted for bonus-point and XP
  purchases, and grant access to discounted Excellencies and Charms.

### 3.1 Specialties (p.77)
- Sub-focus within an Ability (e.g. Socialize: Seduction).
- Bought with bonus points or XP. Max **3 specialties per Ability**.
  **Linguistics is the sole exception — no cap** (p.112); each
  Linguistics specialty is a dialect.
- Optional at creation.

### 3.2 Languages (p.111–112)

- Every character knows **one language family free as her native
  tongue**, with an **automatic specialty in the dialect spoken in her
  homeland** (p.112). This is *not* purchased — Linguistics 0 still
  gives the free native language and dialect specialty.
- **Each dot of Linguistics grants one additional language family.**
  Total = 1 native + Linguistics rating.
- **Literacy** in any known language requires **Lore 1+** (p.111).
- **Linguistics Excellencies do not grant languages** — raising the
  static rating with Charms doesn't increase the count (p.234).

| Language family | Region / use |
|---|---|
| **High Realm** | Dragon-Blooded nobility; all official Realm business |
| **Low Realm** | Realm commoners |
| **Old Realm** | First Age tomes, most spells (oral and written); native tongue of spirits, demons, Fair Folk. **Requires Lore 1+ to purchase.** |
| **Riverspeak** | Scavenger Lands; Guild's working language; lingua franca of traders |
| **Skytongue** | Northern Threshold |
| **Flametongue** | Southern Threshold |
| **Seatongue** | Western Threshold archipelagoes |
| **Forest-tongue** | Eastern Threshold |
| **Guild Cant** | Guild members only; requires **Backing (Guild) 2+** |
| **Tribal Tongues** | Each dot of Linguistics also grants **4 tribal tongues** (separate from the one-family-per-dot rule) |

Dialects within a family are mutually intelligible but instantly mark
the speaker as foreign (p.111). A dialect specialty lets the speaker
pass as a native of that region.

## Step 4 — Advantages (p.77)

### 4.1 Backgrounds (7 dots)

Eleven Backgrounds (p.111–116). At creation **no Background above 3
dots without bonus points**. Each dot beyond 3 costs **2 BP** rather
than 1.

| Background | What it represents | Dot benchmarks |
|---|---|---|
| **Allies** | Close, independent companions roughly as powerful as a starting Exalt | Each dot = 1 ally of starting-Exalt power; higher dots = fewer but stronger |
| **Artifact** | Magical items (weapons, armor, vehicles); may be bought multiple times | Rating = artifact rating 1–5; ratings 4–5 are exceptionally rare |
| **Backing** | Rank in an organization (gov't, army, Guild, crime syndicate) | 1: minor functionary · 3: moderately powerful · 5: one of its leaders |
| **Contacts** | People in various sectors who trade favors | Each dot = 1 major contact + 1 die for minor contacts |
| **Cult** | Worshipers; passive Willpower / Essence regeneration | 1: small cult, +1 temp WP/day · 3: large region, +1 WP/24h reflex + 3m/hr · 5: hunted by the Wyld Hunt. **Solars cap at Cult 2 at creation (p.114).** |
| **Familiar** | Bonded animal that doesn't age while the character lives | 1: ordinary pet · 3: intelligent pet, +5m bonded Essence store, share senses · 5: powerful intelligent pet with Familiar-3 perks |
| **Followers** | Loyal extras (warriors, acolytes, etc.); requires equal Resources/Backing/Influence to maintain | 1: 5 · 2: 20 · 3: 100 · 4: 1,000 · 5: 10,000 |
| **Influence** | Fame and political pull | 1: local · 3: known across neighboring states; Dragon-Blooded notice · 5: rules a nation; the Realm will move on her |
| **Manse** | Controlled demesne / manse generating a hearthstone | n = level-n manse OR level-(n+1) demesne |
| **Mentor** | Patron / teacher who guides and disciplines | 1: slightly more worldly · 3: wise & influential · 5: exceedingly powerful, expects greatness, has enemies |
| **Resources** | Wealth & income | 1: apartment, shoddy gear · 3: townhouse, full kit, servant · 5: bandit king or merchant prince, can rent armies |

The Storyteller may refuse a Background that's inconsistent with the
concept (e.g. Resources for a runaway slave).

### 4.2 Charms (10 picks) (p.77)
- At least **5** must come from Caste or Favored Abilities.
- Each Charm has a minimum Ability + Essence prerequisite; many require
  prerequisite Charms in the same tree.
- **Sorcery exchange:** a character who knows the relevant Sorcery
  Charm (from Occult) may **swap a Charm for one spell of the
  appropriate Circle**. **Solar Circle spells are forbidden at
  creation** (p.77). Terrestrial and Celestial Circle spells are
  permitted via the swap. Spells bought with bonus points cost the
  same as a Charm; the in-caste discount applies if Occult is Caste or
  Favored.
- Common picks: **Ox-Body Technique** (Resistance) — see §5.5. The
  three Excellencies (First / Second / Third) add dice or successes to
  Ability-based rolls — see §6.3 for their hard caps and the two
  general Charms (**Infinite (Ability) Mastery** and **(Ability)
  Essence Flow**) that modify how Excellencies behave.

#### Charm-use rules to internalise *before* picking (p.184–185)

These don't change the 10-Charm budget but constrain how Charms work
together — keep them in mind when selecting trees. Full detail in
`game_rules.md` §8.

- **One Charm per action.** Solars use one Charm per action; reflexive
  Charms count. **Combos** (p.244) are the canonical exception, as is
  **(Ability) Essence Flow** for the three Excellencies of one Ability.
- **Multi-use Supplemental / Reflexive.** The *same* Supplemental or
  Reflexive Charm may be invoked multiple times until next action / DV
  refresh — but only the same Charm.
- **Simple and Extra Action Charms cannot appear in mundane flurries.**
- **(Attribute + Ability) dice cap.** No combination of Charms may add
  more dice than `Attr + Ability` to any roll, or more than
  `⌊(Attr + Ability) / 2⌋` to a static value. Each purchased success
  counts as 2 dice. Automatic successes / penalty removal do not count
  unless the Charm text says so. This is the *single most important*
  long-term constraint on Solar power-level.

### 4.3 Virtues (5 dots)
- 4 Virtues: **Compassion, Conviction, Temperance, Valor**. Each starts
  at 1 free.
- Distribute **5 additional dots** among them.
- No Virtue above **4** at creation without bonus points.
- The character's **primary Virtue** (the highest, at rating **3+**) is
  the root of her **Virtue Flaw** (the Great Curse / Limit Break — see
  §6).

| Virtue | Domain | A failed Virtue check forces… |
|---|---|---|
| **Compassion** | Empathy, mercy, love | Slay a defeated foe, ignore the pleas of the oppressed, humiliate a friend/lover |
| **Conviction** | Endurance, ruthlessness, regaining Willpower | Abandon a cause, give up under hardship, abandon companions |
| **Temperance** | Self-control, clarity, resisting temptation | Act dishonestly, overindulge, act on impulse, break a sworn oath |
| **Valor** | Courage, daring | Turn down a duel/challenge, flee battle, ignore an insult |

**Channelling a Virtue** (p.102): once per story per dot, spend 1
Willpower to add (Virtue rating) **dice** to a related pool. The point
of Willpower spent this way does **not** grant the usual automatic
success — only the bonus dice.

**Suppressing a Virtue** (rating ≥3): the player rolls the Virtue when
the character acts against it. Any success forces compliance or
requires a Willpower point to override. Suppressing the **primary
Virtue** also adds 1 Limit to the Solar.

## Step 5 — Finishing Touches (p.77–78)

### 5.1 Willpower
- **Willpower = sum of the two highest Virtues.**
- **Cap at 8** unless **two or more Virtues are 4+**; absolute maximum
  is **10**.
- Bonus points may raise Willpower; the result still cannot exceed 8
  via bonus points alone unless two Virtues are at 4+ (p.79).

### 5.2 Intimacies (p.88, 173–174)
- Start with **Intimacies = Compassion**.
- Things the character cares about — a cause, ideal, place, person,
  nation — less defining than Motivation but harder to erode than mere
  preferences.
- **Conviction** governs how quickly Intimacies form or shift; building
  or eroding one takes scenes equal to Conviction.
- May be raised at creation up to **Willpower + Compassion** with bonus
  points (3 BP each).

### 5.3 Essence
- Solars start at **Essence 2**.
- Bonus points may raise it (7 BP per dot). Cannot start higher than
  **5**. (Essence 6+ requires being 100+ years old — elder Exalts.)

### 5.4 Essence Pools
- **Personal Essence** = (Essence × 3) + Willpower
- **Peripheral Essence** = (Essence × 7) + Willpower + (sum of all 4
  Virtues)
- Personal Essence spent leaves the anima banner subdued. Peripheral
  Essence spent contributes to the anima display and exposes the
  Exalted's nature once enough is burned in a scene (§2).

### 5.5 Health Levels
- Default 7 levels: **−0, −1, −1, −2, −2, −4, Incapacitated**.
- **Ox-Body Technique** (Resistance, p.209) — stackable up to the
  character's Resistance rating. Each purchase adds, at the player's
  option:
  - **one −0 health level**, OR
  - **two −1 health levels**, OR
  - **one −1 and two −2 health levels**.

## 1. The Five Solar Castes (p.91–101)

Each caste's anima power can be activated by spending the listed motes
as a deliberate action; it **also triggers automatically** when the
Solar spends **11–15 motes of Peripheral Essence** in a scene (the same
threshold that ignites the full anima banner — §2).

### 1.1 Dawn — Bronze Tigers (p.92–93)
- **Caste Abilities:** Archery, Martial Arts, Melee, Thrown, War.
- **Concept:** warrior, general, champion.
- **Anima banner:** brilliant golden sunburst on the brow; aura white
  and pale gold, sometimes tinged red or violet.
- **Anima power (10m):** for the rest of the scene the Dawn appears
  huge and terrifying, with burning eyes and a rending grasp. Any
  creature whose **Valor ≤ the Dawn's Essence** cannot look directly at
  her or strongly oppose her; the Dawn gains **+2 to her DV** against
  affected characters. Mortals (and normal animals) must roll Valor or
  flee in terror. Has **no effect on golems, automata, the walking
  dead, and other creatures that do not know fear**.

### 1.2 Zenith — Pillars of the Sun / The Blasphemous (p.94)
- **Caste Abilities:** Integrity, Performance, Presence, Resistance,
  Survival.
- **Concept:** priest-king, prophet, leader.
- **Anima banner:** large bright-gold circular caste mark; aura
  brilliant white or majestic gold.
- **Anima power, corpse-burn (1m per body, touch):** burns the body of
  a fallen creature to ash in (Essence) ticks, sending soul to Heaven
  and preventing it from rising as zombie or hungry ghost.
- **Anima power, holy radiance (10m, action):** the Zenith glows like
  noon for (Essence × 10) yards. For the rest of the scene (or until
  released) the Zenith gains additional **lethal and bashing soak
  equal to Essence** against attacks from creatures of darkness, AND
  when this power is active her player adds **Essence to the minimum
  number of dice rolled** on any attack against a creature of darkness
  (so most attacks roll at least 2× Essence dice).

### 1.3 Twilight — Copper Spiders / The Unclean (p.95–96)
- **Caste Abilities:** Craft, Investigation, Lore, Medicine, Occult.
- **Concept:** scholar, sorcerer, artisan.
- **Anima banner:** golden circle, top filled gold and bottom an empty
  ring; aura spans brilliant golds plus bright/dark reds, purples, and
  blues — the most spectacular palette of any caste.
- **Anima power (5m, reflexive, after damage):** if a damage roll
  would cost the Twilight one or more health levels, she may spend 5
  motes to **subtract 1 health level of damage per dot of her Essence
  from that single attack**. Can negate a weak attack entirely or
  greatly soften a deadly one.

  > Correction note: previous summaries described this as a flat
  > `Essence × 2 L/B` soak shield over the scene. That is wrong — it is
  > a one-shot, post-damage health-level reduction (p.96).

### 1.4 Night — Concealing Shadows / The Wretched (p.97–98)
- **Caste Abilities:** Athletics, Awareness, Dodge, Larceny, Stealth.
- **Concept:** spy, thief, scout, assassin.
- **Anima banner:** empty golden ring caste mark; aura ghostly white
  and gold, tinged violet and purple-gray.
- **Anima power, anima muting (per-Charm surcharge):** when a Night
  spends Peripheral Essence on a Charm she may pay extra motes so the
  expenditure does **not** add to her anima banner:
  - **+1 mote** for a Charm that is **not Obvious**.
  - **Double the normal mote cost** for an **Obvious Charm** (p.183
    for the Obvious tag).
  - Muting does **not** work on sorcery.
- **Anima power, muted veil (10m, scene):** extends an imperceptible
  muting veil. Senses (sight, smell, sound) and physical traces
  (shadows, footprints, scent) of the Night are dulled — anyone trying
  to notice or track her suffers a penalty equal to her Essence (round
  up) for the scene. Once she spends 11–15 motes of Peripheral
  Essence she becomes as visible as any other Solar in full anima,
  but everything *before* that point remains hidden — observers can
  only confirm "a Solar was here," not her identity.

### 1.5 Eclipse — Crowned Suns / The Deceivers (p.99–100)
- **Caste Abilities:** Bureaucracy, Linguistics, Ride, Sail, Socialize.
- **Concept:** diplomat, censor, oath-binder, intermediary.
- **Anima banner:** golden disc inside a circle; aura brilliant white
  and gold, filmy and fluctuating like an eclipse corona.
- **Anima power, oath sanctification (10m + 1 WP):** the Eclipse seals
  an oath she witnesses or is party to with a handclasp; her anima
  blazes with the words and runes of Heaven's law. A number of times
  equal to the Eclipse's Essence-at-sanctification, any oathbreaker
  (including the Eclipse herself if she breaks it) will horribly botch
  a critical action at the worst possible moment. The Eclipse need not
  even be alive when the curse fires — Heaven enforces it. Becomes
  invocable **without mote cost** once the Solar spends 11–15 motes
  of Peripheral Essence in the scene.
- **Diplomatic protection:** spirits, demon princes, Fair Folk, and
  similar beings who legitimately deal with a recognized Eclipse must
  obey the rules of hospitality. They may refuse business uncompelled,
  but they **cannot attack members of an embassy** unless the Eclipse
  provokes them into breaking the peace.
- **Alien Charm access:** Eclipses may learn Charms of other Exalt
  types and of spirits, the Fair Folk, etc. — see §6.4 below for the
  XP cost caveat.

### 1.6 Universal Solar anima effects (p.92, any caste, 1m each)
- Cause the **caste mark to glow brightly** for a scene (as if 4–7m of
  Peripheral were burned).
- Cause the **anima to glow bright enough to read by** for a scene (as
  if 8–10m of Peripheral were burned).
- **Know the precise time of day** for the rest of the scene.

## 2. Solar Anima Display Levels (p.117)

Driven by motes spent from **Peripheral Essence** in the current
scene; Personal Essence does not contribute. The display resets
between scenes when Essence pools refresh.

| Peripheral motes spent in scene | Display |
|---|---|
| **1–3** | Caste mark glitters and is visible from certain angles. A **Perception + Awareness roll at standard difficulty** spots it; Stealth still works normally. |
| **4–7** | Caste mark burns / shines through coverings. **Stealth Charms cease to function**; mundane Stealth using natural cover suffers **+2 difficulty**. |
| **8–10** | Coruscant aura wreaths the body, bright enough to read by. **Stealth is impossible.** |
| **11–15** | Full bonfire anima from feet to above head, visible for miles. **The caste's anima power activates automatically** (free for the rest of the scene). |
| **16+** | "**Iconic anima**" manifests — a burning totemic image personal to the Solar (great golden bull, sun-mandala, lion, etc.) towers over her. Caste-specific effects persist; visible for miles. |

## 6. Charms (p.77)

### 6.1 Selection rules (10 Charms at creation)
- At least **5** must come from Caste or Favored Abilities.
- Each Charm's **minimum Ability and Essence prerequisites** must be
  met by the character's final traits.
- Many Charms also require **prerequisite Charms** in the same tree;
  walk the tree.

### 6.2 Sorcery exchange
- A character with the relevant Sorcery Charm (from Occult) may swap a
  Charm for one spell of the appropriate Circle.
- **Solar Circle spells are forbidden at creation.** Terrestrial and
  Celestial spells are allowed.
- Spells bought with bonus points cost the same as a Charm (4 BP if
  Occult is Caste/Favored, 5 BP otherwise).

### 6.3 Frequently used Charms

- **Ox-Body Technique** (Resistance) — see §5.5; stackable up to
  Resistance rating.

#### The three Excellencies (p.183, 186–187)

All three: Mins (Ability) 1, Essence 1; Reflexive; Combo-OK; Duration
Instant; no prereqs. Each Ability has its own copy.

| # | Name | Cost | Step | Hard cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Essence Overwhelming | 1m/die | Step 1 attacker / Step 2 defender | Dice added ≤ `(Attr + Ability)`; on static values, each rolled *success* = +1 |
| 2nd | Essence Triumphant | 2m/success | Step 1 attacker / Step 2 defender | Successes added ≤ `⌊(Attr + Ability) / 2⌋` (each counts as 2 dice toward the cap) |
| 3rd | Essence Resurgent | 4m flat | Step 4 attacker / Step 6 defender | Reroll the entire pool, take the better; on static values, adds `⌊Ability / 2⌋` to the relevant DV |

3rd is **incompatible with 1st/2nd on the same roll**.

#### Infinite (Ability) Mastery (p.186–187)
- 2m+ (variable) + 1 WP · Mins (Ability) 4, Essence 3 · **Simple** ·
  Duration: one scene · Prereq: any (Ability) Excellency.
- Each **2m committed** reduces the cost of that Ability's 1st-3rd
  Excellencies by 1m (min 0). Activation motes commit for the scene.
- Commitment cap: Essence 3 → 6m max; Essence 4+ → unlimited.
- Activation is Simple, so it consumes the "one Charm per action" for
  that action.
- **Incompatible with Essence Flow** — does not stack with it.

#### (Ability) Essence Flow (p.187)
- — cost · Mins (Ability) 5, Essence 4 · **Permanent** · Prereq: any
  (Ability) Excellency.
- Lets the Solar invoke that Ability's 1st-3rd Excellencies **as innate
  powers rather than Charms**, so they no longer count as the action's
  one Charm and can fire even inside a Combo that doesn't contain them.
- Still subject to the (Attr + Ability) caps; still cannot apply the
  same Excellency twice to one roll; 3rd still incompatible with 1st/2nd
  on the same roll; cannot use out of order in combat steps.
- Bought **once per Ability** (not per Excellency).
- **Incompatible with any cost-reduction effect** — doesn't stack with
  Infinite Mastery.

### 6.4 Alien Charms (Eclipse only)
Eclipses can learn Charms of spirits, the Fair Folk, and Charms of
other Exalt types **provided they have a tutor** (p.100). The XP cost
appears in the experience-cost table on **p.275** under
"**Non-Solar Charms**", with the footnote
"*Eclipse Caste only: includes spirit Charms and Charms of other
Exalted types.*"

> **Caveat:** the numerical multiplier in our PDF copy of the table is
> OCR-corrupted (rendered "Ai pectit"). The previous summary asserted
> "4× XP" — that figure could not be confirmed from the source we have.
> The Eclipse caste body text on p.100 indicates roughly double the
> normal out-of-caste Solar Charm experience cost. When this matters in
> play, **read the table from a clean copy of the book** rather than
> trusting either number here.

## 8. The Great Curse & Virtue Flaws (p.103–107)

Each flaw entry has four fields:
- **Description** — how the Exalt behaves during Limit Break.
- **Partial Control** — moderated behaviour (the player gives up the
  Willpower bonus to roleplay something less extreme).
- **Duration** — how long the Limit Break lasts.
- **Limit Break Condition** — the trigger that grants Limit dice
  (rolled at Virtue rating).

Limit Break fires automatically at **Limit 10** and resets the meter to
zero. Resisting unnatural mental influence grants **1 Limit** (once
per scene).

### 8.1 Compassion Flaws
- **Compassionate Martyrdom** — throws herself between the suffering
  and the source, willing to absorb harm in their place; will not fight
  unless attacked. **Trigger:** witnesses innocents suffering through
  no fault of their own. **Duration:** 1 scene in combat / 1 day
  otherwise.
- **Heart of Tears** — overwhelmed by grief, weeps, cannot function.
  −2 to defend; flees if possible. **Trigger:** witnesses innocents
  suffering she cannot help. **Duration:** 1 scene / 1 day.
- **Red Rage of Compassion** — charges the obvious cause of suffering,
  ignoring sense, wound penalties, or weapon appropriateness.
  **Trigger:** sees innocents suffering and cannot effectively
  intervene. **Duration:** 1 scene in combat / hours equal to
  Compassion.

### 8.2 Conviction Flaws
- **Deliberate Cruelty** — conscience vanishes; uses terror and
  torture to accomplish goals. −2 dice to non-fear Social rolls.
  **Trigger:** severe stress or being backed against the wall.
  **Duration:** 1 full day.
- **Heart of Flint** — heart hardens; expresses no emotion; chooses
  purely by efficiency. −2 to face-to-face Social rolls.
  **Trigger:** frustrated by the intemperate/childish nature of the
  world. **Duration:** 1 full day.

### 8.3 Temperance Flaws
- **Ascetic Drive** — withdraws from worldly affairs, dresses in
  sackcloth, may destroy possessions / artifacts / manses.
  **Trigger:** confronted by own inadequacies or human weakness.
  **Duration:** 1 full day.
- **Contempt of the Virtuous** — loses respect for others' faults;
  intervenes — physically if needed — to lecture the dissolute.
  **Trigger:** hindered by the self-indulgent. **Duration:** 1 full
  day.
- **Overindulgence** — surrenders to every excess (sex, liquor,
  drugs, gluttony) regardless of cost. **Trigger:** must pass a
  favoured pleasure or vice to act morally. **Duration:** 1 full day.

### 8.4 Valor Flaws
- **Berserk Anger** — attacks anything that moves, hunts whatever
  runs, attacks the scenery if no targets remain. **Trigger:**
  insulted, demeaned, or deliberately frustrated. **Duration:** 1
  full scene.
- **Foolhardy Contempt** — knows no fear; takes the vanguard,
  accepts every challenge, never retreats. **Trigger:** fights at
  losing odds, is challenged to single combat, or has any chance to
  prove bravery. **Duration:** 1 full day.

Storytellers may homebrew flaws of equivalent severity — they must
always be **problematic**.

## 9. Motivation & Intimacies (p.88, p.174)

- **Motivation** — one grand epic goal, **not** a near-term task.
  Cannot be eroded by ordinary social influence; only by life-changing
  events or powerful magic. Acting against Motivation can cost
  temporary Willpower.
- **Intimacies** — emotionally important ties (people, places, causes,
  ideas) less defining than Motivation. Start with **Compassion**
  Intimacies. May be raised at creation to **Willpower + Compassion**
  with bonus points. Building or eroding takes scenes equal to
  Conviction.

## 12. Spark of Life (p.78–79)

Character-description fields the rulebook recognises. The Voidstate
sheet has a slot for each of these (Hair / Eyes / Skin / Features /
Homeland / Sex / Age / Anima Totem) — fill them in here.

| Field | Where defined | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Appearance** | Spark of Life (p.78) | Physical description; let traits inform it. High Strength → visible muscle. High Charisma → confident bearing, steady gaze. High Manipulation → mannerisms and speech. Capture not just looks but dress, posture, voice, and a signature detail (e.g. "massive scar across the left side of her face, gained battling Wyld barbarians"). Enumerate hair / eyes / skin / distinguishing features. |
| **Homeland** | Spark of Life sidebar "You Look Like Some Sort of Foreigner" (p.78) | Has direct mechanical impact — determines **native language family and free dialect specialty** (§3.2, p.112), shapes appearance and customs. Creation has wide diversity: blue hair common in the West, green hair in the West and Haltan Republic, hairless panda-spotted Djala in the South. Re-read the regional chapter (Ch. 1) when picking. |
| **Sex** | Prelude Q&A (p.79) | No mechanical effect; setting treats sex as fully player-determined. Family-life prelude questions assume any sex/gender configuration. |
| **Age** | Prelude Q&A (p.79) | "Almost no Chosen go through their Exaltations before puberty, and most change in their mid-20s. Exaltation rarely happens after 35, but it has been recorded as late as 60." Essence 6+ requires 100+ years (elders only). |
| **Anima Totem** | p.117 (iconic anima) | The burning totemic image that manifests at 16+ Peripheral display (§2). Player's free choice — pick during Step 1, write here. |
| **Social Ties** | Spark of Life (p.78) | Parents, spouse(s), lovers, children, friends, neighbors; slaves / former-slave status. Crucially: **how Exaltation changed each tie** — does her husband now fear her? Do neighbours expect her to fight off tax collectors? |
| **Prelude** | Spark of Life (p.78–79) | One-on-one vignette session run between character creation and first play. Covers mortal life, the Exaltation moment, and immediate aftermath. The Q&A list on p.79 is the canonical prompt list (age, family, homeland, how Exalted, when met the circle, view of mortals, what motivates heroism, religion, what would drive her to murder). |

## 16. Search Cheat Sheet (for `document-search`)

Always scope with `--tag exalted`. Useful queries:

- `"<caste> caste abilities anima powers"` — caste detail.
- `"<Charm name>"` — specific Charm description (Ability min, Essence
  min, cost, type, effect).
- `"<Ability> charms tree"` — Charm tree overview if a single Charm
  search is weak.
- `"<Background> background dots"` — Background trait effects.
- `"<Virtue> virtue flaw"` — Virtue Flaw options.
- `"bonus point costs trait"` — confirm BP table.
- `"sorcery <spell name>"` — sorcery / necromancy spell details (also
  try the Books of Sorcery Vol. 2 — White and Black Treatises).
- For passages, prefer `document-search text "<path>" --pages <a> <b>`
  over guessing.

If similarity is below 0.4, **rephrase rather than guess**.
